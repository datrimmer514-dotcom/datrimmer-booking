## Stories

- **Booking story:** add a **Link** sticker over the "Réserve ici ↓" space, pointing to your booking page.
- **Price list story:** same link sticker; add a **Poll** sticker ("Coupe ou Coupe + Barbe?") to boost engagement.
- Save both to a Highlight named **RÉSERVER** so they stay on your profile.

## Ideas to keep the feed alive

- **Weekly Avant/Après** (use template 3) — every Monday.
- **Reels:** 15-second time-lapse of a cut set to trending audio; first 2 seconds = the final look.
- **"Dans mon kit"** carousel: show your clippers, products, and how you set up in a living room.
- **Client spotlight:** repost a client's story (with permission) + "Merci [nom]!"
- **Disponibilités de la semaine** story every Sunday with open slots and the link sticker.
